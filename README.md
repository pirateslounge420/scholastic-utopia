# Scholastic Utopia

A bright, ambient open world to wander. No items, no goals: just walking through a living
planet and watching the light, the sky and the land change.

The look is **Frutiger Aero** (aqua skies, glossy clouds, clear water, dew, lush greens) and the
feel is a **scholastic, utopian age of exploration**: a naturalist on an expedition through a
world that is beautiful rather than hostile.

Design source of truth: [`docs/DESIGN.md`](docs/DESIGN.md).
